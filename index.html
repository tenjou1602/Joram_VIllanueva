Projects

Runeha ERP

Web App

Problem: Project management, purchasing, warehouse, and accounting ran as separate, disconnected processes. Comparing supplier prices was manual, and there was no clear record of where each project’s funds were going.

Solution: Runeha ERP — a PHP/MySQL platform with Inventory, Sales, Purchasing, and Accounting modules, and dedicated views for Executive Admin, Procurement, Warehouse, and Accounting staff.

My role: Handled database design (ERD and data dictionary), project budgeting and fund requests routed to Accounting, Excel-upload materials lists, automatic supplier price comparison, supplier management, invoicing that pulls in received quotations from Procurement, and PDF invoice reports.

Outcome: A continuous flow from project creation to invoice. Fund requests appear in Accounting automatically, and completed projects are locked from further fund requests and monthly adjustments.
