About Me

I'm Joram Villanueva, a full-stack developer who works across PHP/MySQL backends and Flutter mobile apps. Most of my work starts with a vague requirement — "students need a way to track concerns" or "we need a shop with admin controls" — and ends with a deployed system people can actually use.

I'm strongest when I own a feature end to end: database design, auth, business logic, and the interface. Recent work includes a concern-tracking platform with SLA escalation and role-based dashboards, and a Firebase-backed e-commerce app with cart, orders, and in-app support chat.

I'm currently building my portfolio through OJT and looking for a junior developer or internship role where I can ship features, learn from a team, and keep leveling up on backend and mobile work.
