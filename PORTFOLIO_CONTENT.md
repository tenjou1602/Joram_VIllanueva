## Projects

### 1. Student Concern Tracking System

**Problem:** Students had no clear way to submit concerns, track status, or hold departments accountable for response times.

**Solution:** ConcernTrack — a PHP and MySQL web app with role-based access for students, department staff, and admins.

**My role:** Sole developer. Designed the schema, auth flow, routing rules, SLA auto-escalation job, audit logging, PDF exports, and an FAQ chatbot with optional OpenAI fallback. Built the admin dashboard with escalation rate, average read time, and screening metrics by department.

**Outcome:** A complete workflow from submission through resolution, with automated escalation when SLAs are missed.
